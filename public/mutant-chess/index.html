🧬 Mutant Merge Rulebook

1. Piece Fusion Mechanism
On your turn, instead of making a regular move, you can move one of your pieces onto a square occupied by another friendly piece to merge them into a single "Mutant" piece.

2. Mutant Movement
A Mutant inherits the combined legal movement patterns of both constituent pieces. For example, a Knight + Rook Mutant can leap over pieces like a Knight OR slide in straight lines like a Rook!

3. Fused Queen Symbiosis
When a Rook and a Bishop merge, they transform directly into a Fused Queen (`Q_fused`) with a unique glowing purple aura.

4. Fusion Rules & Restrictions
• Fusion Pool: Players have a limited number of Fusions per game (Default: 3, configurable up to 15).
• King Fusions: Room creator can enable or disable King Fusions.
• Capacity: A maximum of 2 pieces can occupy a single square.
• Single-Merge Limit: Fused pieces cannot be merged a second time.
• No Duplicate Types: You cannot merge two pieces of the same type (e.g. Knight + Knight).
• Forbidden Unions: Queens cannot merge with Rooks, Bishops, or Pawns. Kings cannot merge with Pawns.

5. Pawn Promotions
Only pure, non-fused Pawns reaching the 8th rank can promote. Fused Pawns cannot be promoted.

6. Instant Victory
Capturing the opponent's King (or any Mutant containing the enemy King) results in an immediate victory!
