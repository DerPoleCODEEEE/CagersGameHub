🔮 Prediction Chess Rulebook

1. It is normal chess.
All the usual rules apply — castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate and stalemate. There is no chess clock. Instead you get 90 seconds per move, refilled every turn.

2. Call your opponent's next move.
Calling their reply is mandatory: you cannot move until you have drawn an arrow (right-click drag, or select two squares with Enter) showing the move you expect from your opponent. Your arrow and your move are sent together, so a fast opponent can never beat you to it. The only exception is the second move of a Double Move — the call from your first move is still open there.

3. Hits pay coins.
Only an exact match counts — same starting square, same target square. A hit pays 1 coin, multiplied by your streak: ×1.5 from 3 in a row, ×2 from 5, ×3 from 8. A miss pays nothing and resets your streak.

4. Both arrows are revealed.
Once your opponent has moved, your arrow is shown to both of you — green for a hit, red for a miss. They learn how you read them, and can start moving against your habits.

5. Spend coins on items.
Buying an item uses it immediately — there is no inventory. You may buy only on your own turn, only one item per turn, and never while you are in check. Your opponent sees every purchase, unless you cloak it.

6. Nothing in the shop wins the game for you.
No item delivers mate, freezes a king, or plays your opponent's move for them. Every effect has a visible duration, and an item that would leave your opponent with no legal move at all is refused by the server.
