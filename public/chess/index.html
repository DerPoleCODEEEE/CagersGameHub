⚡ Quick Chess Rulebook

1. Real-Time Gameplay (No Turns)
Quick Chess discards traditional turn-based play. Both players move their pieces simultaneously in real time as soon as individual piece cooldowns expire.

2. Game Modes
• Class Lock: Moving a piece puts ALL pieces of that same type (e.g. all Pawns or all Rooks) on cooldown.
• Single Piece: Moving a piece puts ONLY that specific moved piece/square on cooldown.
• Blitz Single: Every piece shares a flat 2.0s cooldown regardless of type.

3. Piece Cooldown Timers (Standard)
• ♚ King: 1.0s
• ♟ Pawn: 3.5s
• ♞ Knight & ♝ Bishop: 6.5s
• ♜ Rook: 10.0s
• ♛ Queen: 14.0s

4. Piece Travel & Locking
Pieces physically move across the board over a short duration (250ms–750ms based on distance). While a piece is traveling, its destination square is locked.

5. Victory Condition (King Capture)
There is NO Check or Checkmate! The sole objective is to physically capture the enemy King. First player to take the opponent's King wins instantly.

6. Special Moves
• Castling: Permitted if King and Rook have not moved and path is empty.
• En Passant: Valid immediately after an opponent pawn advances two squares.

7. Disconnect & Forfeit
If a player disconnects mid-match, a 30-second forfeit timer starts. Reconnecting within 30 seconds resumes the match seamlessly.
